Honest notes

  • The v7 weight-divergence lesson. sov33-v7 measured 0.581 held-out on T4 but 0.290 on M4 (Δ +0.291). A same-named model on two substrates is not the same model unless the weights join. That is why the crosscheck join is weights-verified, not name-verified.
  • LOST-WEIGHTS register. Eight custom-weight models (sov33-dist-c1..c3, sov33-evolved-c1/c3, sov33-evolved, sov33-v6, sov33-evolved-patched) were honestly declared LOST after a blob-store wipe on 2026-08-02. They are absent here rather than silently substituted: a model NAME is not a model.
  • Salt-split reproducibility. RunPod A4500 control runs v1 (runpod-v1-control-saltcheck) and v2 (runpod-v2-salt-baseline) share corpus root 3729b52e…3ef01d (417 provisions). The anti-Goodhart salt re-splits practice/held-out between runs, so per-split accuracies move while the corpus anchor holds — reproducibility is proven at the anchor level, not by split leakage.
  • 17 of 57 joined models diverge (|Δ| > 0.15) between T4 and M4 (mean signed Δ 0.096, median 0.097). Substrate is a measurement variable; SwarmBench reports it, always.
