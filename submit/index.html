Submit your mixture for measurement

MoE model, mixture-of-models team, swarm, clan, council — if a group of models produces verdicts together, SwarmBench will measure what the group actually adds. Including when the answer is less than one.

What we need from you

1. Model list

Every member, with weights identity (hash or verifiable artifact reference). We join on weights, not names — a renamed model is a new model.

2. Routing / voting policy

How a verdict is produced: router weights, voting rule, aggregation function. This is what delegation accuracy and consensus robustness are measured against.

3. Split-policy agreement

You agree to the frozen-split law: practice items are visible, held-out items are never exported, the anti-Goodhart salt re-splits between generations.

The measurement contract

  • We publish with CI and harness. Scores ship with 95% confidence intervals, coverage fields (measured / unparseable / UNMEASURED), the exact harness version, and the corpus anchor hash. No number without provenance.
  • You get a signed attestation. Every completed measurement is sealed via the live trust spine (os.meok.ai/api/sign, verifiable at os.meok.ai/api/verify) — a signed attestation of what was measured, when, and against which corpus root.
  • Negative results publish too. If your council measures n_eff below its seat count, that is the finding. It joins the Illusion Index next to ours. You can withdraw before publication; you cannot edit after.
